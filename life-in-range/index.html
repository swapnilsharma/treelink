Your bloodstream, as a loop

Orange dots are glucose. More dots = higher blood sugar. That's the number on your CGM.

Blue dots are insulin, entering at the ring — where your pump or pen delivers it.

While you digest, the stomach streams glucose in. Insulin lets muscle and fat pull it back out. The pancreas stays dark — in type 1, it can't help.
